Hi! I am a senior undergraduate majoring in Computer Science and Technology at the [University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS)](https://www.ucas.ac.cn/). I am currently exploring AI agents and recommender systems under the guidance of [Prof. Fei Sun](https://ofey.me/) at [STAR Group](https://ict-star.github.io/).

My research interests lie in the **reliability and safety of large language models and LLM-based agents**, as well as **recommender systems and recommendation agents**. My earlier project experience spans computer systems and software development. Beyond coursework, I participated in two iGEM seasons as a software developer; [our team](https://2025.igem.wiki/ucas-china/) received Silver and Gold Medals. In Spring 2026, I studied at the University of California, Berkeley as a visiting student, and explored prompt-based attacks and defenses through literature reading and discussions.

Outside of my academic work, I’m passionate about music and puzzles. I enjoy listening to music, playing the piano, and singing in a choir—activities that give me both a creative outlet and a sense of community. I also love tackling puzzles of all kinds, taking part in puzzle hunts, and designing puzzles of my own. These interests keep me curious, patient, and energized, and they often offer a fresh perspective on my academic work.
